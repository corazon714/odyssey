Odyssey — art direction bake-off, round 2

Three directions in the widened space: modern, kinetic, spatial. Four screens each at 375px, plus a fifth panel that moves — the signature transition — because a static mockup cannot show a kinetic direction. Copy is verbatim from packages/content/; the deltas, DC 12 and the d20 are the real values. The resource strip is icon + number, per your decision — a pressure gauge (hunger, heat) is marked by a hatched track, a shape difference rather than a hue, so it survives colourblindness.

Round 1 is retired, and so is the brief that produced it. The retro-print reference list and the “should not feel bouncy” instruction are recorded as WITHDRAWN in docs/art-direction.md §1, so a later session cannot revive them. Two findings from round 1 survive because they are facts about the game rather than about those directions: German body copy runs +50% over English (RU +17%), and the eight-cell resource strip cannot hold a German label at 375px.
The German and Russian here are mockup copy and have not been reviewed by a native speaker. packages/content/i18n/{de,ru,tr}/ are empty. These strings live in this file only, are not seeded into the locale, and exist to make the layout honest — the German carries real compounds (Grenzabfertigungsbeamte, Aufenthaltsgenehmigungen) and the Russian exercises Cyrillic at body, chip and tabular-numeral sizes.
